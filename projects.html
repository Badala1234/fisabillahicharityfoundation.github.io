What We Do

Fisabillahi Charity Foundation is committed to helping people in need through charity, education, healthcare, empowerment and other humanitarian initiatives.

Our projects are designed to bring hope, dignity and meaningful opportunities to vulnerable members of our communities.

01

Orphan Support

Supporting orphans and vulnerable children with essential needs and opportunities that can improve their lives.

02

Education Support

Helping children from families with limited resources access quality education, Islamic learning and useful educational materials.

03

Food Assistance

Providing food and essential supplies to families and individuals experiencing hardship.

04

Healthcare Support

Supporting people who need assistance with healthcare and humanitarian medical needs.

05

Business Empowerment

Supporting people with limited means through empowerment initiatives that can help them become more financially independent.

06

Emergency Assistance

Responding to urgent humanitarian needs and providing assistance to people facing difficult circumstances.

Upcoming Initiatives

In Sha Allah, we plan to expand our humanitarian work and reach more people who need support.

Supporting Children in Education

We aim to identify orphans and children from financially disadvantaged families who are unable to attend school or Islamic learning programmes.

Where possible, we hope to provide educational materials, connect them with suitable schools and support their educational expenses.

Community Empowerment

We hope to develop more sustainable empowerment projects that can help individuals and families build a better future.
