Education & Orphan Support Initiative

One of our important goals is to identify vulnerable children, particularly orphans and children whose families have limited financial means, and support them in accessing education.

Where resources allow, assistance may include educational materials, school-related expenses and other necessary support.

  • Identify children who need assistance
  • Provide essential learning materials
  • Support access to appropriate education
  • Encourage Islamic and moral education
  • Monitor the impact of the assistance

How We Work

We aim to ensure that charitable resources reach people who genuinely need assistance.

1

2

3

Support

4

Follow Up
