Our Projects

Turning compassion into meaningful action and bringing hope to people who need it most.

What We Do

Fisabillahi Charity Foundation works to support vulnerable people and communities through practical humanitarian programmes.

Our projects focus on education, orphan support, healthcare, food assistance and economic empowerment.

01

Orphan Support

Supporting orphans and vulnerable children with essential needs, care and opportunities for a better future.

02

Education Support

Helping children from disadvantaged families access Islamic and formal education, school materials and other educational needs.

03

Healthcare Assistance

Providing support to people facing medical challenges and helping vulnerable families access necessary care.

04

Food Assistance

Providing food and essential supplies to families and individuals experiencing hardship.

05

Business Empowerment

Supporting people with limited resources through opportunities that can help them become more self-reliant.

06

Humanitarian Assistance

Responding to urgent needs and supporting vulnerable members of our community during difficult times.
