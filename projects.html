Our Projects

Serving humanity by supporting vulnerable people, education, healthcare, environmental protection and sustainable community development.

Our Areas of Work

Fisabilillahi Charity Foundation is committed to supporting people in need and contributing positively to our communities.

🎓

Education Support

Supporting children and young people who face financial difficulties by helping with school materials, educational opportunities and access to learning.

❤️

Orphan Support

Supporting vulnerable orphans with essential needs and seeking opportunities to improve their education, wellbeing and future.

🏥

Healthcare Support

Assisting vulnerable people with healthcare needs and supporting humanitarian health interventions whenever resources allow.

🍚

Food & Basic Needs

Providing support to vulnerable families and individuals who need help with food and other essential needs.

🤝

Business Empowerment

Supporting people with limited resources through small business opportunities and sustainable means of livelihood.

🌍

Humanitarian Assistance

Responding to humanitarian needs by supporting vulnerable individuals and communities whenever possible.

🌳

Tree Planting & Environmental Protection

Promoting tree planting and environmental protection to help create a greener, healthier and more sustainable community for present and future generations.

🏘️

Community Development

Supporting initiatives that encourage cooperation, improve community wellbeing and contribute to positive social development.
