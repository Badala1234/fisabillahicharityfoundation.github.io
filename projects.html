Our Humanitarian Projects

Fisabillahi Charity Foundation works to support vulnerable people through practical charitable projects that respond to genuine community needs.

01

Orphan Support

Supporting orphans with essential needs, educational materials and other forms of assistance.

02

Education Support

Helping children from families with limited resources access Islamic and formal education.

03

Food Assistance

Providing food and basic necessities to vulnerable families and individuals in need.

04

Healthcare Support

Supporting vulnerable people who need assistance with healthcare and medical-related needs.

Empowerment & Sustainable Support

We believe charity should not only provide immediate assistance but should also help people become stronger and more self-reliant.

Where possible, we aim to support small opportunities, skills and initiatives that can help individuals and families build a better future.

More Projects Are Coming

With the support of generous donors and volunteers, we hope to reach more people and expand our humanitarian activities, in sha Allah.
