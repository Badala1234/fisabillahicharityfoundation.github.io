Who We Are

Fisabillahi Charity Foundation is a humanitarian initiative dedicated to helping people in need and supporting communities through charitable and meaningful activities.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, hope and the opportunity to build a better future.

Our work is inspired by compassion, transparency, responsibility and the desire to serve humanity for the sake of Allah.

01

Our Mission

To support vulnerable people and create practical opportunities that improve lives.

02

Our Vision

To build a compassionate community where people help one another and no vulnerable person is forgotten.

03

Our Values

Compassion, honesty, transparency, responsibility and service to humanity.

04

Our Commitment

To use charitable support responsibly and focus on meaningful assistance for those who need it.

What We Do

Our humanitarian activities include supporting orphans, promoting education, assisting people with healthcare needs, providing food assistance and empowering people through sustainable opportunities.

We also aim to respond to genuine community needs and support projects that can create lasting positive impact.

Be Part of the Good

Every act of kindness can make a difference. Together, we can reach more people and bring hope to those who need it most.
