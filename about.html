About Fisabilillahi Charity Foundation

Serving humanity for the sake of Allah by supporting vulnerable people, orphans, education, healthcare, empowerment and humanitarian causes.

Who We Are

Fisabilillahi Charity Foundation is a humanitarian initiative dedicated to bringing hope, dignity and meaningful support to people who need it most.

🤲 Our Foundation

Fisabilillahi Charity Foundation was established with the aim of serving humanity and supporting vulnerable members of our communities.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, compassion and an opportunity to build a better future.

Through charitable contributions and humanitarian projects, we seek to provide practical assistance where it is most needed.

❤️ What We Believe

We believe that even a small act of kindness can make a meaningful difference in someone's life.

Our work is guided by compassion, accountability, fairness, transparency and the desire to serve humanity for the sake of Allah.

We aim to create sustainable opportunities rather than providing temporary assistance alone.

Our Mission & Vision

Everything we do is guided by our commitment to humanitarian service and positive community impact.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide meaningful humanitarian assistance to vulnerable people, support orphans and disadvantaged children, promote education, assist people facing healthcare challenges, and empower individuals and families through sustainable opportunities.

🌍

Our Vision

To build a compassionate society where vulnerable people receive support, children have access to education, families can become self-reliant and communities work together to improve the lives of those in need.

Our Main Objectives

Our humanitarian activities focus on areas where charitable support can create lasting positive impact.

🤲

Support for Orphans

Supporting vulnerable orphans with essential needs, education and other forms of assistance.

📚

Education

Helping children from disadvantaged families access learning materials, Islamic education and appropriate schooling opportunities.

🏥

Healthcare Assistance

Providing support to people facing healthcare challenges and helping vulnerable families access essential medical assistance.

💼

Economic Empowerment

Supporting people with opportunities that can help them develop skills, businesses and sustainable sources of income.

🍚

Food Assistance

Supporting vulnerable households with food and essential household provisions when needed.

🏠

Humanitarian Support

Responding to humanitarian needs and providing assistance to individuals and families facing difficult circumstances.
