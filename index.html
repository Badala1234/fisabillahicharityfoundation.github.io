About Us

Fisabilillahi Charity Foundation is dedicated to serving humanity through compassion, charity and sustainable community support.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support people in need, especially orphans and vulnerable members of society, while creating opportunities for education, healthcare and economic empowerment.

Our Vision

We envision a stronger and more caring community where vulnerable people receive support and every child has an opportunity to learn, grow and build a better future.

Our Areas of Support

Your support helps us reach people and families who need assistance.

❤️

Orphan Support

Supporting orphans with essential needs, educational materials and other assistance.

📚

Education

Helping children access quality education, school materials and Islamic learning.

🏥

Healthcare

Supporting people facing healthcare needs and humanitarian emergencies.

💼

Business Empowerment

Helping vulnerable people develop sustainable means of earning a living.

🤝

Humanitarian Services

Providing assistance to individuals and families facing difficult circumstances.

🕌

Community Support

Building a culture of compassion, cooperation and helping one another for the sake of Allah.

Together, We Can Make a Difference

A small contribution can become a great source of hope for someone in need.
