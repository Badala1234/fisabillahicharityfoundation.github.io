About Our Foundation

Who we are and what we stand for

Serving Humanity for the Sake of Allah

Fisabilillah Charity Foundation is committed to improving the lives of vulnerable people through compassion, community support and sustainable humanitarian activities.


Our work focuses on supporting orphans, promoting education, assisting people in need of healthcare, empowering low-income individuals and providing humanitarian services.


Location: Gumel, Jigawa State, Nigeria.

CAC Registration Number: 9694272
