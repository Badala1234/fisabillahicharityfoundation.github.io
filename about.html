Who We Are

Compassion in Action

Fisabilillahi Charity Foundation is a humanitarian organization committed to serving people in need and strengthening communities through practical and meaningful initiatives.

Our work is guided by compassion, integrity, responsibility and the belief that helping others can create lasting positive change.

Our Guiding Purpose To serve humanity for the sake of Allah and help create opportunities for people to live with dignity, hope and purpose.

Our Direction

Mission & Vision

Our mission guides what we do today, while our vision inspires the future we want to help build.

🎯

Our Mission

To support vulnerable people, promote education and healthcare, empower individuals, protect the environment and provide humanitarian assistance with compassion, dignity and responsibility.

🌍

Our Vision

To contribute to stronger communities where vulnerable people have access to support, children have opportunities to learn, individuals can build sustainable livelihoods and everyone is treated with dignity.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

The principles below guide our decisions, relationships and humanitarian activities.

01

Compassion

We care about people and seek to respond to genuine needs with kindness and humanity.

02

Integrity

We value honesty, transparency and responsible stewardship in our work.

03

Dignity

We believe every person deserves respect, regardless of their circumstances.

04

Service

We focus on practical action that can positively affect people's lives.

05

Responsibility

We aim to use available resources wisely and direct them toward meaningful impact.

06

Community

We believe sustainable change is stronger when communities work together.
